About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Entomology, School of Agriculture, Mohan Babu University, Tirupati, India. My principal research interests are insect taxonomy and systematics, with particular emphasis on Lepidoptera of agricultural importance.

My research focuses on the identification and characterization of lepidopteran species using adult morphology, wing venation, male and female genitalia, DNA barcoding and phylogenetic approaches. I am particularly interested in Pyraloidea, Tortricoidea, Gracillarioidea and Noctuoidea, including species associated with economically important agricultural and horticultural crops.

Research Contributions

  • Taxonomic and systematic studies on agriculturally important Lepidoptera.
  • Morphological characterization of moths using adult habitus, wing venation and male and female genitalia.
  • Contribution to the description of Spinaria indica and Herpetogramma viraktamathi.
  • Documentation of the occurrence of Thaumatotibia zophophanes in India and its association with litchi.
  • Application of DNA barcoding and molecular approaches to support morphological identification of insect species.
  • Research on insect pests of cereals, horticultural crops and other economically important plants.

Publications

My research publications primarily focus on insect taxonomy and systematics, Lepidoptera, agricultural entomology, biodiversity, pest biology and integrated pest management.
